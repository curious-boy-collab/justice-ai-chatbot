## 1. Supreme Court of India: Approved Strength and Constitutional Basis
- **Approved / Sanctioned Strength:** 34 Judges (including the Chief Justice of India) pursuant to the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Act, 2019.
- **Constitutional Basis:** Judges of the Supreme Court of India are appointed by the President of India under Article 124(2) of the Constitution based upon recommendations tendered by the Supreme Court Collegium.
- **Collegium Composition:** The Chief Justice of India and the four senior-most puisne Judges of the Supreme Court.
- **Retirement Age:** 65 years.
- **Working Strength Dynamics:** Working strength changes with retirements, elevations, and appointments. Citizens must always verify current day-to-day vacancies on the official Department of Justice portal.

---

## 2. High Courts: Structure, Sanctioned Strength and Vacancies
- India currently possesses **25 High Courts** exercising jurisdiction across 28 States and 8 Union Territories.
- **Total Approved / Sanctioned Strength:** 1,114 Judges nationwide across all 25 High Courts (consisting of Permanent Judges and Additional Judges).
- **Appointment Procedure:**
  - Governed by the Memorandum of Procedure (MoP) framed pursuant to the Second and Third Judges Cases.
  - The High Court Collegium (comprising the Chief Justice of the respective High Court and two senior-most judges) initiates recommendations.
  - Constitutional consultation is undertaken with the Chief Minister and the Governor of the State.
  - The Union Minister of Law & Justice processes the dossier, coordinates intelligence inputs, and submits it to the Supreme Court Collegium (CJI + two senior-most judges).
  - Upon approval, the recommendation is forwarded through the Prime Minister to the President of India, who issues warrants of appointment under Article 217 or Article 224.
- **Retirement Age:** 62 years.
- **Vacancies Notice:** Judge vacancies fluctuate continuously due to superannuation, elevation to the Supreme Court, transfers, or new appointments. Never invent current vacancy statistics.

---

## 3. District and Subordinate Judiciary
- **Jurisdictional Framework:** District and Subordinate Courts are under the administrative superintendence and control of the respective High Courts and State Governments under Articles 233, 234, and 235 of the Constitution.
- **Sanctioned vs Working Strength (National Benchmark):**
  - Total Sanctioned Strength: Approximately 25,000 judicial officers nationwide.
  - Total Working Strength: Approximately 19,000 to 20,000 judicial officers.
  - Recruitment is conducted through State Public Service Commissions and High Court Judicial Recruitment Boards.

## 4. Strict Anti-Hallucination Rule & Live Official Verification
- **IMPORTANT CITIZEN RULE:** Never invent or guess current statistics for judge vacancies or appointments. Because presidential appointments and retirements occur throughout the calendar year, current statistics must be cross-checked on official websites:
- **Monthly Vacancy Statements:** Published monthly on [https://doj.gov.in](https://doj.gov.in) under the "Judges / Vacancy Statement" tab.
- **Live National Judicial Data Grid:** Real-time judge strength and court-wise pendency data is updated continuously on [https://njdg.ecourts.gov.in](https://njdg.ecourts.gov.in).
