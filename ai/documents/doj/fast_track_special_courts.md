# Fast Track Special Courts (FTSCs)

> **Category:** Fast-Track Justice & POCSO Cases

> **Retrieval Date:** 2026-11-08  
> **Verification Status:** Verified Official Government Data

---

## 1. Background and Legislative Mandate
In the aftermath of the Criminal Law (Amendment) Act, 2018, which introduced stringent penalties for sexual offences against children and women, the Union Cabinet approved a Centrally Sponsored Scheme to set up **Fast Track Special Courts (FTSCs)** across the nation.

The scheme is administered by the Department of Justice, Ministry of Law & Justice, in coordination with State Governments and High Courts, funded partly through the **Nirbhaya Fund**.

---

## 2. Core Objectives
- Expeditious trial and disposal of pending rape cases under Section 376 of the Indian Penal Code (now corresponding sections of Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita).
- Dedicated adjudication of offences under the **Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012**.
- Minimizing victim trauma through child-friendly courtrooms, vulnerable witness deposition complexes, and swift disposal within statutory timelines.

---

## 3. Implementation and Operational Scale
- **Approved Target:** Setup of 1,023 Fast Track Special Courts across India, including 389 exclusive POCSO Courts.
- **Current Operational Courts:** Over 750+ FTSCs actively functioning across participating States and Union Territories (as per latest Department of Justice progress reports).
- **Specialized Features:**
  - Dedicated Special Judges and Special Public Prosecutors.
  - Video-recorded testimony facilities to shield child victims from the accused.
  - Periodic case monitoring directly mapped on the National Judicial Data Grid (NJDG).

---

## 4. How Citizens and Litigants Can Access Information
- Victims and families can contact the District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) or Protection Officers under the POCSO Act.
- Case tracking for cases assigned to FTSCs can be done on the eCourts Services portal and mobile app using the unique 16-digit CNR number.
- Official scheme performance reports are updated at [https://doj.gov.in/fast-track-special-courts/](https://doj.gov.in/fast-track-special-courts/).
