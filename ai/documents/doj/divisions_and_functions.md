## 1. Overview and Constitutional Mandate
The Department of Justice (DoJ) is part of the Ministry of Law & Justice, Government of India. It performs administrative, advisory, and developmental functions related to the administration of justice in India in accordance with the Allocation of Business Rules, 1961.

The DoJ acts as an institutional bridge between the Union Executive and the Judiciary, respecting the constitutional independence of the higher judiciary while facilitating budgetary, technological, and infrastructural assistance.

## 2. Key Administrative Divisions

### A. Appointment Division (Judicial Appointments)
- Facilitates the appointment and conditions of service of the Chief Justice of India, Judges of the Supreme Court of India, Chief Justices, and Judges of all 25 High Courts in India.
- Coordinates the constitutional collegium process under Articles 124, 217, and 224 of the Constitution of India.
- Handles matters relating to the resignation, retirement, salaries, pensions, and leave rules of higher judiciary judges.

### B. eCourts Project Division (ICT & Modernization)
- Formulates, monitors, and implements the flagship eCourts Mission Mode Project in partnership with the eCommittee of the Supreme Court of India.
- Oversees computerization, cloud infrastructure, video-conferencing facilities, and digital connectivity (WAN/LAN) across thousands of district and subordinate court complexes.
- Manages key public portals: eCourts Services, National Judicial Data Grid (NJDG), eFiling, ePay, and Virtual Courts.

### C. Access to Justice (A2J) & Legal Aid Division
- Implements policies and schemes to enhance access to justice for poor, marginalized, vulnerable groups, women, children, and undertrial prisoners.
- Operates the **DISHA Scheme** (Designing Innovative Solutions for Holistic Access to Justice in India), integrating Tele-Law, Nyaya Bandhu (pro bono legal services), and Nyaya Mitra initiatives.
- Coordinates with the National Legal Services Authority (NALSA) and State Legal Services Authorities (SLSAs).

### D. Judicial Infrastructure Division
- Administers the Centrally Sponsored Scheme (CSS) for the Development of Infrastructure Facilities for the Judiciary.
- Provides central funding assistance to State Governments and Union Territories for constructing court halls, residential accommodations for judicial officers, digital litigant waiting rooms, and vulnerable witness rooms.

### E. Fast Track Special Courts (FTSC) Division
- Monitors and supports the implementation of Fast Track Special Courts across States and UTs dedicated to the expeditious trial of cases under the POCSO Act and rape offences.
