## 1. Background, Constitutional Mandate, and Enactment
Pursuant to the Criminal Law (Amendment) Act, 2018—enacted to impose rigorous punishment and create deterrence against sexual violence—the Union Government approved a flagship Centrally Sponsored Scheme to establish **Fast Track Special Courts (FTSCs)** nationwide.

The scheme is administered by the Department of Justice, Ministry of Law & Justice, in close collaboration with respective High Courts and State Governments, funded centrally under the **Nirbhaya Fund** established for women's safety.

---

## 2. Statutory Mandate and Target Timeline
- **Core Focus Area 1 (POCSO Act):** Dedicated adjudication of offences under the **Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012**.
- **Core Focus Area 2 (Rape Cases):** Expeditious trial of rape cases under Section 376 IPC / corresponding sections of Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS).
- **Statutory Mandate on Timeline:** The Criminal Law (Amendment) Act and POCSO Act mandate completion of investigation within 2 months and completion of trial within **1 year (12 months)** from the date of taking cognizance.

---

## 3. Funding Ratio and Financial Architecture
- **Nirbhaya Fund Funding Formula:**
  - **General States:** 60% Central Government share, 40% State Government share.
  - **North Eastern and Himalayan States (Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, UT of J&K):** 90% Central Government share, 10% State Government share.
  - **Union Territories without legislature (e.g., Andaman & Nicobar, Ladakh, Chandigarh):** 100% funded by Central Government.

---

## 4. Specialized Infrastructure and Victim Care
1. **Exclusive POCSO Courts:** Districts with more than 100 pending POCSO cases are earmarked for Exclusive POCSO Courts headed by a dedicated Special Judge.
2. **Vulnerable Witness Deposition Centres (VWDC):**
   - Safe, non-intimidating, child-friendly waiting rooms and deposition facilities.
   - One-way glass screens and audio-video links preventing the child victim from coming face-to-face with the accused during testimony.
   - Presence of support persons, child psychologists, and protection officers.
3. **Dedicated Public Prosecutors:** Special Public Prosecutors assigned exclusively to fast-track matters to eliminate adjournments.

---

## 5. Approved Scheme Scale vs Working Strength
- **Approved Scheme Target:** 1,023 Fast Track Special Courts across India, including 389 exclusive POCSO Courts.
- **Operational Progress:** Over 750+ FTSCs are actively operational across participating States and Union Territories (refer to DoJ monthly progress updates).
- **Anti-Hallucination Reminder:** Never invent current disposal numbers or active court numbers. Live operational figures should be confirmed on the official Department of Justice portal at [https://doj.gov.in/fast-track-special-courts/](https://doj.gov.in/fast-track-special-courts/).

---

## 6. How Victims and Litigants Can Access Support
- Contact the **District Legal Services Authority (DLSA)** located within every District Court complex for free legal representation.
- Track case progress using the 16-character CNR number on the eCourts Services portal or mobile app.
- For emergency and victim assistance, dial the National Women Helpline (**181**) or Childline (**1098**).
