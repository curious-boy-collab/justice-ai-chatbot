## 1. What are Virtual Courts for Traffic Offenses?
Virtual Courts are an initiative under the eCourts Project administered by the Department of Justice and the eCommittee of the Supreme Court of India.

A Virtual Court is presided over by a judicial officer virtually, allowing petty offences—such as traffic violations and compoundable motor vehicle infractions—to be adjudicated completely online without requiring the presence of the citizen or advocate in physical courtrooms.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step: How to Settle a Traffic Challan via Virtual Courts
When an e-challan issued by the Traffic Police is transferred to a Virtual Court:

1. Visit the official Virtual Courts portal: [https://vcourts.gov.in](https://vcourts.gov.in).
2. Select your **State** and **Department / Court** (e.g., *Delhi - Notice Department* or *Maharashtra - Virtual Court*).
3. Search for your case using one of the following:
   - **Mobile Number**
   - **CNR Number**
   - **Challan Number** (from the traffic SMS/receipt)
   - **Vehicle Registration Number**
   - **Party Name**
4. View the violation summary, date, time, photographic evidence, and proposed fine determined by the Virtual Court Judge.
5. You are presented with two legal options:
   - **Plead Guilty:** Accept the offence and proposed fine. Proceed immediately to online payment via credit/debit card, net banking, or UPI. Upon payment, the case is officially marked **Disposed**, and an electronic receipt is issued.
   - **Contest / Do Not Plead Guilty:** If you dispute the violation or wish to challenge the fine, click *Contest*. The case is automatically transferred to the regular jurisdictional regular physical court for hearing.

---

## 3. Alternative: Direct Parivahan eChallan Settlement
Before a challan is transferred to court, it can often be paid directly on the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) portal:
1. Visit [https://echallan.parivahan.gov.in](https://echallan.parivahan.gov.in).
2. Click **Check Challan Status**.
3. Enter Challan Number, Vehicle Number, or Driving Licence Number.
4. Complete payment via payment gateway and download receipt.
