## 1. What are Virtual Courts for Traffic Offenses?
Virtual Courts are an innovative digital initiative spearheaded by the eCommittee of the Supreme Court of India and the Department of Justice under the eCourts Mission Mode Project.

A Virtual Court is presided over virtually by a designated Metropolitan Magistrate or Judicial Magistrate First Class. The primary objective is the electronic adjudication and instant settlement of petty offences—primarily traffic violations issued under the Motor Vehicles Act—without requiring physical footfall of the violator, lawyer, or police officer in a physical courtroom.

## 2. Step-by-Step Procedure: How to Settle a Traffic Challan Online
When a traffic violation is captured by traffic cameras, speed sensors, or handheld traffic police devices, an e-challan is registered and transferred to the Virtual Court server. Litigants can settle it through the following workflow:

1. **Visit the Virtual Courts Portal:** Open [https://vcourts.gov.in](https://vcourts.gov.in).
2. **Select Jurisdiction:** Select your **State** and **Department / Court** (e.g., *Delhi Notice Department*, *Maharashtra Virtual Court*, *Tamil Nadu Traffic Virtual Court*).
3. **Search for Violation Record:** Search using any one of the following identifiers:
   - **Mobile Number** (registered at the time of challan issuance)
   - **Challan Number** (from traffic SMS or physical notice)
   - **Vehicle Registration Number** (e.g., DL01AB1234)
   - **16-Digit CNR Number**
   - **Party Name**
4. **Review Evidence and Proposed Fine:** The portal displays the violation summary, date, time, camera photographic proof, and the fine amount proposed by the Virtual Court Judge.
5. **Select Adjudication Action:** The citizen is given two legal options:
   - **Option A: Plead Guilty & Pay Fine Online:**
     - The citizen admits the offence and accepts the proposed fine.
     - Choose the payment gateway (SBI ePay, Bharatkosh, Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, or UPI).
     - Once payment is successful, an electronic acknowledgement receipt is generated.
     - The case status changes to **Disposed**, the traffic offence is marked settled, and the vehicle record is updated on the national database.
   - **Option B: Contest / Dispute Challan:**
     - If the citizen claims they did not commit the infraction, were not driving, or have valid statutory grounds to challenge the notice, click **Contest**.
     - The case is immediately redirected out of the virtual court and transferred to the regular physical jurisdictional regular court for a standard trial.

## 3. Alternative: MoRTH Parivahan eChallan Settlement
Before a traffic challan is transferred to the judicial Virtual Court server, it can frequently be settled directly with the enforcement authority:
1. Visit the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) portal: [https://echallan.parivahan.gov.in](https://echallan.parivahan.gov.in).
2. Click on **Check Online Services > Check Challan Status**.
3. Enter Challan Number, Vehicle Number, or Driving Licence (DL) Number.
4. Verify OTP sent to registered mobile.
5. Pay through the integrated payment gateway and print receipt.
