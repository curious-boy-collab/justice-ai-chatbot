## 1. Overview and Constitutional Mandate
The Department of Justice (DoJ) is an integral administrative department of the Ministry of Law & Justice, Government of India. In terms of the Allocation of Business Rules, 1961, the Department performs vital functions concerning the administration of justice in India.

The Department functions as an institutional interface between the Union Executive and the Judiciary. While firmly upholding and respecting the constitutional independence of the higher judiciary, the Department facilitates judicial appointments, provides budgetary and capital investment support, modernizes court processes through ICT, and expands access to justice for vulnerable communities.

## 2. Key Administrative Divisions and Their Specific Responsibilities

### A. Appointment Division (Judicial Appointments & Conditions of Service)
- Administers the constitutional process for appointment, elevation, and transfer of the Chief Justice of India, Judges of the Supreme Court of India, and Chief Justices and Judges of all 25 High Courts across the nation under Articles 124, 217, and 224 of the Constitution.
- Coordinates the collegium recommendations and the executive vetting workflow governed by the Memorandum of Procedure (MoP).
- Handles matters concerning judicial pensions, post-retirement benefits, leave rules, salaries, and official notifications published in the Gazette of India.

### B. eCourts Project Division (ICT, Modernization & Digital Justice)
- Implements the flagship Pan-India eCourts Mission Mode Project under the National e-Governance Plan, monitored jointly with the eCommittee of the Supreme Court of India.
- Deploys hardware, high-speed secure networking (WAN), cloud hosting, and video-conferencing units across more than 18,700 courtrooms and 3,000+ court complexes nationwide.
- Formulates Phase III of the eCourts Project with a focus on paperless digital courts, open APIs, AI-assisted transcription, and citizen-first digital access.
- Operates digital citizen platforms: eCourts Services Portal, National Judicial Data Grid (NJDG), eFiling 3.0, ePay, Virtual Courts, and the NSTEP mobile application for electronic bailiff process service.

### C. Access to Justice (A2J) & Legal Aid Division (DISHA Scheme)
- Implements policies and centrally sponsored initiatives to guarantee equal access to justice under Article 39A of the Directive Principles of State Policy.
- Spearheads the **DISHA Scheme** (Designing Innovative Solutions for Holistic Access to Justice in India), unifying three transformative citizen initiatives:
  1. **Tele-Law:** Pre-litigation legal counseling through Common Services Centres (CSCs).
  2. **Nyaya Bandhu (Pro Bono Legal Services):** Connecting volunteer advocate champions with eligible marginalized litigants.
  3. **Nyaya Mitra:** Assisting district courts in disposing of cases pending for more than a decade.
- Coordinates closely with the National Legal Services Authority (NALSA) and State Legal Services Authorities (SLSAs).

### D. Judicial Infrastructure Division
- Manages the Centrally Sponsored Scheme (CSS) for the Development of Infrastructure Facilities for the Judiciary.
- Disburses central financial grants to State Governments and Union Territories for constructing modern courtrooms, residential complexes for judicial officers, lawyer consultation halls, digital litigants waiting rooms, and vulnerable witness deposition complexes.
- Funding formula: 60:40 ratio for general States, 90:10 ratio for North Eastern and Himalayan States, and 100% for Union Territories without legislature.

### E. Fast Track Special Courts (FTSC) Division
- Implements and monitors Fast Track Special Courts dedicated to expeditious disposal of cases under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act and rape offences under the Indian Penal Code / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita.
- Supported under the Nirbhaya Fund to ensure time-bound judicial proceedings within 1 year.

### F. Administration, Budget, and National Judicial Academy (Judicial Training)
- Coordinates administrative oversight of the National Judicial Academy (NJA) at Bhopal for continuous judicial education, ethics, and capacity development of judges.
- Handles parliamentary affairs, audit compliances, public grievances through CPGRAMS, and the Right to Information (RTI) Cell.
