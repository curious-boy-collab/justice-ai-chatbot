# Live Streaming and Recording of Court Proceedings

## 1. Background and Constitutional Foundation
In the landmark judgment *Swapnil Tripathi v. Supreme Court of India (2018)*, the Supreme Court held that live streaming of court proceedings is an extension of the **Open Court principle** enshrined under Article 21, Article 19(1)(a), and Article 145(4) of the Constitution of India.

The eCommittee of the Supreme Court subsequently framed the model **Rules for Live Streaming and Recording of Court Proceedings** to guide High Courts across India.

---

## 2. Supreme Court of India Live Streaming
- Constitutional Bench hearings and matters of national significance are live streamed directly on the Supreme Court official YouTube channel and the dedicated Supreme Court web portal ([https://main.sci.gov.in](https://main.sci.gov.in)).
- Full archived video recordings and audio transcripts (including AI-assisted real-time transcription powered by natural language models) are hosted for public educational access.

---

## 3. High Courts Live Streaming Initiatives
Several High Courts have adopted formal rules and stream their court halls daily, including:
- High Court of Gujarat (Pioneer in public streaming)
- High Court of Karnataka
- High Court of Madhya Pradesh
- High Court of Orissa
- High Court of Patna
- High Court of Calcutta
- High Court of Delhi

Official streams are hosted on verified official High Court YouTube channels and institutional portals.

---

## 4. Exceptions and Restrictions (Matters NOT Streamed)
To protect privacy, national security, and vulnerable witnesses, the following matters are strictly exempted from live streaming:
1. Matrimonial and family disputes (divorce, custody, domestic violence).
2. Offences under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act.
3. Sexual assault and rape proceedings under Section 376 IPC / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita.
4. Juvenile justice hearings.
5. In-camera proceedings and matters where the presiding judge directs confidentiality in the interest of justice.

---

- Official YouTube channel of the Supreme Court of India.
