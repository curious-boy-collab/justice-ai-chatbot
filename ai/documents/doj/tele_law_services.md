## 1. What is the Tele-Law Initiative?
Tele-Law is a pioneering digital legal empowerment initiative conceptualized by the Department of Justice, Ministry of Law & Justice, Government of India, under the umbrella **DISHA Scheme** (Designing Innovative Solutions for Holistic Access to Justice in India).

Launched in 2017 in strategic partnership with Common Services Centres (CSC) e-Governance Services India Limited and the National Legal Services Authority (NALSA), Tele-Law democratizes access to justice by connecting disadvantaged citizens living in remote villages with qualified Panel Lawyers via video-conferencing, telephone calls, and the Tele-Law Mobile App.

---

## 2. Who is Entitled to 100% Free Legal Advice?
Under **Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987**, the following vulnerable and disadvantaged categories receive **completely free** pre-litigation legal counseling:
1. **Women and Children.**
2. **Members of Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST).**
3. **Victims of Human Trafficking, Beggar Systems, or Bonded Labour.**
4. **Persons with Disabilities / Differently-Abled Persons.**
5. **Victims of Mass Disasters, Ethnic Violence, Caste Atrocities, Floods, Earthquakes, Droughts, or Industrial Accidents.**
6. **Industrial Workmen / Labourers.**
7. **Persons in Custody, Under-Trials, or Residents of Juvenile Homes / Protective Facilities.**
8. **Low-Income Citizens:** Individuals whose annual income falls below the statutory ceilings fixed by respective State Legal Services Authorities.
*(General category citizens not covered under Section 12 can access legal consultations for a nominal fee fixed by CSC guidelines).*

## 3. How Citizens Can Access Tele-Law Services

### Method A: Through Common Services Centres (CSCs) in Gram Panchayats
1. The citizen visits their nearest village Common Services Centre (CSC).
2. The **Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE)** or **Para-Legal Volunteer (PLV)** logs onto the Tele-Law portal ([https://www.tele-law.in](https://www.tele-law.in)).
3. The citizen's personal particulars, contact number, and brief description of the legal grievance are entered.
4. A convenient video-conferencing or telephone appointment is booked with a State Legal Services Authority (SLSA) Panel Lawyer.
5. During the consultation, the Panel Lawyer advises the citizen on rights, remedies, document preparation, and whether the case can be mediated or requires formal litigation. If formal representation is required, the lawyer refers the matter to the nearest District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) for free court counsel.

### Method B: Tele-Law Citizen Mobile Application
- Citizens can download the **Tele-Law Mobile App** on Android Google Play Store or iOS App Store.
- Allows direct self-registration, appointment scheduling, uploading dispute documents, and consulting lawyers directly from home without visiting a CSC.

### Method C: National Citizen Helpline
- Citizens can dial **14416 (Toll-Free)** to obtain information, guidance, and appointment assistance.

## 4. Key Scheme Statistics and Geographic Reach
- Operational across all 36 States and Union Territories.
- Networked across more than 2.5 lakh Gram Panchayats and Common Services Centres.
- Millions of pre-litigation consultations facilitated, resolving disputes peacefully before reaching congested courtrooms.
