## 1. What is eFiling 3.0?
eFiling Version 3.0 is a standardized nationwide electronic case filing system designed by the eCommittee of the Supreme Court of India in collaboration with the National Informatics Centre (NIC). It enables advocates and party-in-person litigants to file cases electronically into High Courts and District Courts across India 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.

eFiling eliminates physical paper filings, cuts carbon footprints, eliminates long registry queues, and accelerates case scrutiny.

## 2. Step-by-Step Procedure for eFiling

### Step 1: User Registration and Profile Verification
1. Visit the official portal: [https://efiling.ecourts.gov.in](https://efiling.ecourts.gov.in).
2. Choose account type:
   - **Advocate Registration:** Requires Bar Council Enrollment Number, state bar council affiliation, registered mobile number, and email. Mobile OTP verification is compulsory.
   - **Party-in-Person (Litigant) Registration:** For citizens filing cases without a lawyer, verified via Aadhaar KYC or government photo ID.
3. Set secure credentials and enable two-factor authentication.

### Step 2: Case Details and Party Information Entry
1. Select the target **State**, **District**, and **Court Establishment** (or High Court bench).
2. Select the Case Type (e.g., Civil Suit, Writ Petition, Criminal Appeal, Special Leave Petition).
3. Input detailed complainant/petitioner and respondent/accused particulars (names, addresses, age, mobile numbers).
4. Specify the governing Acts and Sections.

### Step 3: Document Preparation and Upload
1. Pleadings, Vakalatnama, affidavits, memo of parties, and annexures must be converted to **searchable PDF/A format** (OCR compliant).
2. Apply digital verification:
   - **Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC token)**, OR
   - **Aadhaar-based eSign OTP** service.
3. Upload documents into designated slots (Main Petition, Impugned Order, Vakalatnama, Annexures).

### Step 4: Fee Payment and Final Submission
1. Calculate the required court fee and pay online via the integrated **ePay** portal.
2. Review the consolidated electronic draft dossier.
3. Click **Final Submit**.
4. An **eFiling Acknowledgement Number** is generated instantly.
5. The Court Registry examines the filing for defects (scrutiny stage). Once approved, a regular Case Number and 16-character CNR number are generated and notified via SMS/email.

## 3. ePay: Online Court Fee and Judicial Deposit Platform
- **Official Portal:** [https://pay.ecourts.gov.in](https://pay.ecourts.gov.in)
- Integrated seamlessly with:
  - State Treasury portals (e-GRAS, Cyber Treasury, RajKosh, Koshwani).
  - Bharatkosh (Central Treasury portal).
  - SBI Multi Option Payment System (SBI MOPS).
- **Accepted Payments:**
  - Court Fees and Judicial Stamp Duty.
  - Process Fees (for bailiff summons and notices).
  - Judicial Fines, compounding fees, and cash bail deposits.
  - Maintenance deposits in family court cases.
- **Payment Instruments:** UPI (BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, Debit Cards, and Credit Cards.
- **e-Court Fee Verification:** Every ePay transaction generates an electronic receipt containing a unique Transaction Reference Number and barcoded e-Court Fee stamp that can be verified online by court scrutiny officers.
