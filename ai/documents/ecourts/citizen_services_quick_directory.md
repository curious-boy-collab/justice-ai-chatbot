## 1. Quick Citizen Services Overview
The Department of Justice, Ministry of Law & Justice, Government of India, offers a suite of digital citizen services under the eCourts Mission Mode Project and DISHA scheme. Citizens can access judicial information, pay fines, obtain free legal counseling, and track case dockets without visiting physical court complexes.

---

## 2. Directory of Key Citizen Portals and Services

### A. Case Status & Cause Lists
- **Official Web Portal:** [https://services.ecourts.gov.in](https://services.ecourts.gov.in)
- **Primary Search Key:** 16-character alphanumeric CNR number.
- **Alternative Search Options:** Party name, case number, advocate name, FIR number.
- **SMS Status:** SMS `ECOURTS <16-digit CNR>` to **9766899899**.

### B. Traffic Violation Fine Settlement (Virtual Courts)
- **Official Web Portal:** [https://vcourts.gov.in](https://vcourts.gov.in)
- **Procedure:** Select State > Search Challan or Vehicle Registration Number > View evidence > Plead Guilty and Pay Online via UPI/Cards/Netbanking, or click Contest to transfer to physical court.
- **MoRTH Parivahan Portal:** [https://echallan.parivahan.gov.in](https://echallan.parivahan.gov.in).

### C. Free Pre-Litigation Legal Advice (Tele-Law)
- **Official Web Portal:** [https://www.tele-law.in](https://www.tele-law.in)
- **Toll-Free Helpline:** Dial **14416**.
- **Physical Access:** Visit any of 2.5 lakh+ Common Services Centres (CSCs) across Gram Panchayats.
- **Eligibility for Free Counsel:** Under Section 12 of Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987 (Women, Children, SC/ST, victims of disasters, persons with disabilities, low income).

### D. Court Fee & Judicial Deposits Payment (ePay)
- **Official Web Portal:** [https://pay.ecourts.gov.in](https://pay.ecourts.gov.in)
- **Payable Items:** Court fees, judicial process fees, fines, penalty deposits.
- **Payment Modes:** UPI, Debit/Credit Card, Internet Banking through State Treasury e-GRAS and Bharatkosh.

### E. Electronic Case Filing (eFiling 3.0)
- **Official Web Portal:** [https://efiling.ecourts.gov.in](https://efiling.ecourts.gov.in)
- **Eligibility:** Enrolled Advocates and Party-in-Person litigants.
- **Features:** 24x7 online filing, digital signing (DSC / Aadhaar eSign), registry scrutiny status tracking.

### F. Watch Live Court Hearings
- **Supreme Court Live Stream:** [https://streaming.sci.gov.in](https://streaming.sci.gov.in) and official YouTube channel.
- **High Courts Live Streams:** Available on official YouTube channels of High Courts (Gujarat, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Orissa, Patna, etc.).
- **Prohibited Cases:** Family disputes, POCSO, rape trials, and in-camera matters are exempted from live streaming.

### G. National Judicial Data Grid (NJDG)
- **Official Web Portal:** [https://njdg.ecourts.gov.in](https://njdg.ecourts.gov.in)
- **Features:** Real-time nationwide case pendency, disposal trends, civil vs criminal breakdown, age-wise case classification.

## 3. Physical Citizen Access Points: eSewa Kendras
For citizens who lack internet connectivity, computers, or smartphones:
- **eSewa Kendras** operate inside High Courts and District Court complexes nationwide.
- They offer free assistance: checking case status, downloading orders, handling eFiling, scanning documents, and providing links to virtual court hearings.
