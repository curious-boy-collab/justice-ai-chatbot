## 1. What is a CNR Number?
The **Case Number Record (CNR)** is a unique 16-character alphanumeric code assigned to every single case filed across any District or Subordinate Court in India.

### Decoding the 16-Character CNR Format
Example: `DLHC01-001234-2024` (or written without dashes as `DLHC010012342024`)
- **Characters 1–2 (State Code):** Two letters representing the State or Union Territory (e.g., `DL` for Delhi, `MH` for Maharashtra, `UP` for Uttar Pradesh, `KA` for Karnataka).
- **Characters 3–4 (District Code):** Two letters designating the judicial district or High Court establishment (e.g., `HC` for High Court, `SW` for South West Delhi, `PU` for Pune).
- **Characters 5–6 (Court Complex Code):** Two numeric digits identifying the specific court complex within that district.
- **Characters 7–12 (Case Filing Serial Number):** Six numeric digits representing the chronological registration sequence of the case in that calendar year (e.g., `001234`).
- **Characters 13–16 (Year of Filing):** Four numeric digits designating the year when the matter was instituted (e.g., `2024`).

Because the CNR number is nationally unique across the entire Indian judiciary, entering it retrieves the exact court docket immediately without needing to identify the state, district, or court name.

## 2. Step-by-Step Procedure: How to Check Case Status Online

### Method A: Search by 16-Digit CNR Number (Fastest and Most Accurate)
1. Open the official web portal: [https://services.ecourts.gov.in](https://services.ecourts.gov.in) or open the **eCourts Services Mobile App**.
2. Select **CNR Number Search**.
3. Type the 16-digit CNR without spaces, slashes, or dashes.
4. Solve the visual/audio captcha challenge.
5. Click **Search**.
6. **Information Rendered:**
   - **Case Details:** Filing Date, Registration Date, Case Type, Case Number.
   - **Case Status:** First Hearing Date, Decision Date (if disposed), Case Stage (e.g., Evidence, Arguments, Judgment), Presiding Judge, Court Hall Number.
   - **Petitioner and Respondent Details:** Names and addresses of parties.
   - **Advocates Details:** Names of appearing counsels.
   - **Hearing History (Business of Court):** Complete date-wise hearing history with downloadable PDF copies of interim orders, proceedings, and final judgments.

### Method B: Search by Case Number (When CNR is not known)
1. Select the relevant **State**, **District**, and **Court Complex**.
2. Select the Case Type (e.g., Original Suit, Criminal Misc, Motor Accident Claim).
3. Enter the Case Number and Year of Registration.
4. Complete captcha and click Search.

### Method C: Search by Party Name (Petitioner / Respondent)
1. Select State and District.
2. Enter the Petitioner's or Respondent's Name.
3. Select the registration year (or check "Pending / Disposed").
4. Click Search to display matching case dockets.

### Method D: Search by FIR Number (Criminal Cases)
1. Select Police Station from the dropdown.
2. Enter FIR Number and Year of lodging.
3. Fetch the linked criminal trial status.

### Method E: Advocate Name or Bar Council Registration
- Litigants can search all active matters handled by their advocate by entering the lawyer's name or Bar Council enrollment number.

## 3. Offline & Mobile Alternative Channels
- **SMS Pull Service:** Send `SMS ECOURTS <16-character CNR>` to **9766899899** from any mobile number to receive instant hearing dates and case stage by SMS.
- **eSewa Kendras:** Available in all District Court complexes for free assistance and printed order sheets.
