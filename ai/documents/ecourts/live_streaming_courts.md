## 1. Constitutional Foundation and Landmark Precedent
In the historic judgment *Swapnil Tripathi v. Supreme Court of India (2018) 10 SCC 639*, the Supreme Court held that live streaming of court hearings is an integral extension of the **Open Court principle** guaranteed under:
- **Article 21:** Right to fair trial and access to justice.
- **Article 19(1)(a):** Freedom of speech and right to know.
- **Article 145(4):** Requirement that Supreme Court judgments and proceedings be conducted in open court.

Under the leadership of the Chief Justice of India and the Supreme Court eCommittee, formal **Model Rules for Live Streaming and Recording of Court Proceedings (2021)** were framed and circulated to all High Courts.

## 2. Supreme Court of India Live Streaming Architecture
- **Official Streaming Portal:** [https://streaming.sci.gov.in](https://streaming.sci.gov.in)
- **Official YouTube Channel:** Streamed directly through the verified Supreme Court of India channel on YouTube.
- **Scope of Streaming:** Constitution Bench hearings, full court ceremonial sessions, matters of national importance, and key constitutional challenges are streamed in real time.
- **AI-Assisted Transcription:** The Supreme Court employs natural language processing and speech-to-text models to transcribe arguments of senior advocates and bench questions in real time, creating transparent public records for legal researchers and citizens.

## 3. High Courts Live Streaming Initiatives
Several High Courts across India have framed High Court Live Streaming Rules and stream their daily court proceedings publicly:
- **High Court of Gujarat:** The pioneer High Court to initiate regular public courtroom streaming.
- **High Court of Karnataka:** Broadcasts multiple division benches and single-judge courts daily.
- **High Court of Madhya Pradesh:** Streams principal bench at Jabalpur and benches at Gwalior and Indore.
- **High Court of Orissa:** Provides live court viewing across multiple benches.
- **High Court of Patna:** Broadcasts daily constitutional and criminal proceedings.
- **High Courts of Calcutta, Delhi, Gauhati, and Telangana:** Provide live and archived proceeding access.

## 4. Strict Legal Exceptions: Matters Prohibited from Live Streaming
To safeguard individual privacy, dignity of victims, state security, and fair administration of justice, the Model Rules strictly prohibit live streaming of:
1. **Matrimonial and Family Disputes:** Divorce proceedings, child custody battles, adoption, and maintenance claims.
2. **POCSO Act Offences:** Any matter involving offences against children under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012.
3. **Sexual Offences and Rape Trials:** Proceedings under Section 376 IPC / corresponding sections of Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, where revealing the identity of the victim is illegal.
4. **Juvenile Justice Proceedings:** Any inquiry or appeal concerning a child in conflict with law.
5. **In-Camera Hearings & National Security:** Official Secrets Act proceedings, anti-terror cases, or any matter where the presiding judge issues confidentiality directions.
6. **Strict Prohibition on Unauthorized Recording:** Unauthorized downloading, screen-recording, editing, meme creation, or commercial distribution of live court streams is strictly prohibited and constitutes criminal contempt of court.
