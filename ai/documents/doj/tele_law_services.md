## 1. What is Tele-Law?
Tele-Law is an innovative flagship program under the **DISHA Scheme** of the Department of Justice, Ministry of Law & Justice, launched in 2017 in partnership with the Common Services Centres (CSC) e-Governance Services India Limited and the National Legal Services Authority (NALSA).

It connects marginalized, disadvantaged, and remote citizens directly with Panel Lawyers stationed at State Legal Services Authorities through video-conferencing, audio-calling, and the Tele-Law Mobile App.

---

## 2. Who is Entitled to Free Legal Advice?
Under Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, the following categories receive **100% free legal advice**:
- Women and children.
- Members of Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST).
- Victims of human trafficking or beggar systems.
- Persons with disabilities / differently-abled persons.
- Victims of mass disasters, ethnic violence, caste atrocities, flood, drought, earthquake, or industrial disaster.
- Industrial workers.
- Persons in custody or in protective homes / juvenile facilities.
- Low-income citizens (annual income thresholds prescribed by respective State Governments).
- Other citizens can access the service for a nominal fee fixed by CSC guidelines.

---

## 3. How Citizens Can Access Tele-Law

### Method A: Through Village Level Entrepreneurs (VLE) at Common Service Centres (CSCs)
1. Citizen visits their nearest village CSC kiosk.
2. The Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE) or Para-Legal Volunteer (PLV) enters citizen details and brief case nature on the Tele-Law portal.
3. An appointment slot is scheduled with a Panel Lawyer.
4. On the scheduled appointment, the citizen speaks with the lawyer via secure video conference or phone call.
5. The lawyer provides professional, pre-litigation legal advice, explaining next steps, document requirements, and whether legal representation via Legal Services Clinics or DLSA is required.

### Method B: Tele-Law Citizen Mobile Application
- Available for direct download on Android Google Play Store and iOS App Store.
- Allows direct self-registration, appointment booking, document attachment, and consultation without visiting a physical center.

---

## 4. Key Metrics and Milestone Achievements
- Tele-Law is operational in all 36 States and Union Territories.
- Integrated across more than 2.5 lakh Gram Panchayats and Common Service Centres nationwide.
- Over 60 lakh+ pre-litigation legal consultations facilitated since inception.
- Toll-free grievance helpline and support available via the official portal.
