## 1. What is eFiling?
eFiling (Version 3.0) is a digital platform designed by the eCommittee of the Supreme Court of India and NIC that enables advocates and party-in-person litigants to file cases electronically into Indian High Courts and District Courts 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.

It completely eliminates physical paper filings, long queues at court registry filing counters, and delays in scrutiny.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Procedure for eFiling

### Step 1: User Registration
1. Visit [https://efiling.ecourts.gov.in](https://efiling.ecourts.gov.in).
2. Register as an **Advocate** (requires Bar Council Enrollment Number, verification via OTP on registered mobile/email) or as a **Litigant / Party-in-Person**.
3. Set login credentials.

### Step 2: Case Details Entry
1. Select the relevant State, District, and Court Establishment.
2. Choose Case Type (e.g., Civil Suit, Writ Petition, Criminal Appeal).
3. Enter details of Petitioner/Complainant and Respondent/Accused (names, addresses, contact details).
4. Specify the relevant legal Acts and Sections.

### Step 3: Document Upload
1. Upload the petition/plaint, vakalatnama, affidavits, annexures, and supporting evidence.
2. Documents must be in searchable PDF format (OCR compliant).
3. Apply Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) or Aadhaar-based eSign.

### Step 4: Verification and Token Generation
1. Complete digital verification.
2. An initial **eFiling Acknowledgement Number / Token** is generated for tracking Registry scrutiny.
3. Once approved by the Court Scrutiny Officer, a permanent Case Number and 16-digit CNR number are allocated.

## 3. ePay: Online Court Fee Payment
- **Official Portal:** [https://pay.ecourts.gov.in](https://pay.ecourts.gov.in)
- Integrated with State Treasury e-Gras portals and Bharatkosh.
- Litigants and lawyers can pay:
  - Court Fees
  - Judicial Stamp Duties
  - Fines, Penalties, and Maintenance Deposits
- Payment Methods: Net Banking, Debit Cards, Credit Cards, and UPI (BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).
- Digital receipt with a unique transaction reference number is generated instantly to be attached to the eFiling docket.
